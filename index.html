Garaged is a vehicle maintenance record for people who service their own cars and motorcycles. It is built so that your records stay with you. This page says exactly what leaves your phone, why, and what does not.

The short version

  • Your vehicles, jobs, parts, photographs and receipts are stored on your phone and, if you choose, in a backup folder you pick. They are not sent to us.
  • To use the app you create an account with an email address and a password. That is the only personal information we hold about you.
  • When you add a vehicle by registration number, and from time to time afterwards to pick up a new MOT, that number is sent to the DVSA MOT History service to fetch the vehicle's details and MOT history. We do not keep a record of it.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting in the app.
  • Location data in photographs is removed at the moment of capture. The app never reads or stores your location.

Why an account at all, if your records stay on the phone? Because the vehicle lookup below spends a request against a government API quota issued in our name, and it has to be able to tell one user from another to limit how often it is called. Nothing else about the account is used for anything.

Vehicle lookup

When you add a vehicle by its registration number, the app sends that number to a small service we run, which forwards it to the DVSA MOT History API and returns the vehicle's make, model, colour, fuel type, engine size, registration dates, MOT test results and recorded mileages. That data is then stored on your phone.

The same lookup runs again from time to time, so that a new MOT test — its result, its mileage reading and any advisories — reaches your record without you having to re-enter it. This happens at most once a day for a vehicle, when you open that vehicle in the app, and whenever you tap Check for a new MOT yourself. It does not run in the background, and it does not run for a vehicle you have marked as sold.

  • Our service does not store the registration number or the result. It holds a short-lived count of lookups per account, in memory, to limit how often the service can be called.
  • The hosting provider's function logs may record that a request happened, and when, for a short period.
  • The DVSA receives the registration number in order to answer the lookup. The DVSA is a government body and is a separate data controller for what it does with requests it receives.
  • MOT data is public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. It is reproduced in the app and in the sale report with that attribution.

You can add a vehicle by hand instead, without a lookup, and nothing is sent.

Everything else stays on your device

The following are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Vehicles, registration numbers, VINs, nicknames, purchase details
  • Jobs, parts, part numbers, costs, notes, torque figures and fluid specifications
  • Service schedules and reminders
  • Photographs, receipts and other documents you attach
  • Text read from receipts by the on-device text recognition (see below)
  • Search indexes built from the above

None of this is transmitted to us. The app has no server-side copy of your records.

Backup to a folder you choose

You can set up a backup to a folder of your choosing on your phone or in a storage provider you already use (for example a folder synced by a cloud storage app). The app writes your records and documents there in plain, readable files so that they outlive the app.

  • You choose the folder through Android's own file picker. The app receives access to that one folder and nothing else on your device or in your provider account.
  • The backup is not encrypted by the app. It is your data in your storage, alongside the photographs your camera already puts there. If you want it encrypted, choose a provider or folder that encrypts at rest.
  • Whatever your storage provider does with files you place in it is governed by that provider's policy, not this one.

Photographs, receipts and text recognition

  • Photographs are compressed and re-encoded on the device when captured. That process removes GPS location data. The date the photograph was taken is read out of the image and kept as a plain field, because it helps prove when work was done.
  • Receipt scanning and text recognition run entirely on the device, using Google's ML Kit components supplied through Google Play services. No image and no recognised text leaves your phone for this purpose. Google Play services may download the recognition module on first use; that download is governed by Google's terms.
  • Every correction you make to a recognised part number or brand is kept on your phone so the app recognises it next time. This learned dictionary is not sent to us.

The sale report

You can generate a PDF report of a vehicle's history to hand to a buyer. It is produced on your phone. It is shared only when you share it, through Android's share sheet, to a recipient you choose. Location data is stripped from every image in it. We never receive a copy.

Reminders

Reminders for planned jobs are optional, set per job, and delivered by your phone's own local notification system. No reminder is sent from a server. The app asks for notification permission the first time you set a reminder, and not before.

What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not read or store your location.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone, except as described above for the vehicle lookup.
  • We do not send marketing email. The only emails you receive are the ones you trigger: account confirmation and password reset.

How long we keep things

  • Your account, and the email address attached to it, until you delete the account.
  • Lookup service logs: for the short retention period applied by the hosting provider.
  • Your records on the phone: until you delete them in the app, or uninstall the app, or clear its data.
  • Your backup folder: until you delete it. It is yours, and uninstalling the app does not remove it.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and object to or restrict our processing of it. Because the only personal data we hold is your account details, these requests are simple to meet.

Your maintenance records are already in your hands. The app's export produces every record and document in open formats (JSON, CSV and the original image files) at any time, without asking us.

To exercise any right, contact aoo.garaged@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app, under Settings. Deleting it removes the account, the email address it is under, and everything held against it on our server. This cannot be undone.

You can also delete your account without the app: contact aoo.garaged@gmail.com from the email address on the account, and we will remove it.

Either way, deleting your account does not touch the records on your phone or in your backup folder. Those are yours to keep or remove. The registration lookup stops working until you create a new account, and the rest of the app keeps working on the records already on the phone.

Children

Garaged is for people who own and maintain vehicles. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly hold an account for anyone under 13.

Changes

If the app starts sending records to a server — for example if an optional sync feature is added in future — that will be a change to this policy, published with the version of the app that introduces it, and described in the app before it is switched on for you.
